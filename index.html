Case 01. Equipment Cover DFM Optimization

Case 01. 運轉機構維修蓋 DFM 優化

Manufacturing Translator 製造翻譯

Overcoming deep drawing constraints to secure dynamic mechanisms.

降維打擊：突破深抽成型限制，確保動態機構穩定。

The client designed a maintenance port on a cover to inspect internal gears and electronics. Their original deep-drawing approach was prone to forming failures. We translated the design into a two-piece stamped assembly, resolving formability issues while perfectly guaranteeing zero oil leakage and no additional noise during operation.

Case 03. 太陽能支架模組改良設計

Identifying field pain points to engineer a smarter, scalable solution.
